About the Crop Video

Crop Video keeps one rectangle for the whole duration and downloads that region as an MP4. Use 9:16 or 4:5 when a landscape clip needs a vertical frame, 1:1 for a square, or 16:9 to drop empty bars. Freeform is any shape. Custom takes a ratio you type. Drag the box, drag a handle, or type x, y, width, and height. The highlighted rectangle is the crop, in pixels, so the file matches the preview.

The preview player is muted so you can line the frame up. The exported MP4 keeps the soundtrack when the file has one. Width and height are even because the encoder uses 4:2:0. The whole duration is cropped, not a single frame. Nothing is uploaded, and no account is required. A long video can be slow, and a phone may run out of memory. The first run downloads the ffmpeg engine.

Where people use the Crop Video

  • Cutting a landscape clip down to a 9:16 frame.
  • Keeping a square region for a thumbnail-style loop.
  • Removing empty space at the edge of a screen recording.

How to use the Crop Video

  1. 1

    Add a video

    Choose a file. A crop box appears on the preview once the dimensions are known.

  2. 2

    Set the region

    Drag the box, drag a handle, or type X, Y, width, and height. Free is any shape. 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, and 4:5 lock the shape. Custom takes a ratio of your own.

  3. 3

    Read the pixel line

    The line under the preview is the exact rectangle the encoder will keep. The box on the picture uses those same pixels.

  4. 4

    Crop and download

    Encoding runs locally. The result preview shows the cropped MP4, and you can download it.

Tips and best practices

  • Scrub the preview to a frame where the subject is in the box before you export.
  • If the box jumps by a pixel, that is the even-size snap. The readout is what you will download.

Frequently asked questions

Does the file match the box I see?

Yes. The box and the encoder use the same pixel rectangle. Width and height are even because the encoder uses 4:2:0.

Is the video uploaded?

No. The crop runs in your browser. The preview is muted; the download still includes the audio track when there is one.

What if the clip is long?

The whole duration is cropped, not just one frame. A long video can take a while, and a phone can run out of memory. Keep the tab open until the result appears.

Can I crop to a vertical frame?

Yes. 9:16 and 4:5 are built in, and Custom takes any ratio. Free lets you drag any rectangle.
