About the Rotate PDF Online

Rotate PDF Online turns pages by 90, 180, or 270 degrees. You can rotate the whole document or only the pages you select. The turn is added to any rotation already stored in the file, then the new PDF downloads. Nothing is uploaded.

Where people use the Rotate PDF Online

  • Fixing a scan that came out sideways.
  • Turning one landscape page inside a portrait document.

How to use the Rotate PDF Online

  1. 1

    Choose a PDF

    The page count appears after the file loads.

  2. 2

    Pick a turn

    Choose 90, 180, or 270 degrees.

  3. 3

    Choose the pages

    Leave All pages on, or turn it off and tap the page numbers. Long documents use a page range such as 1-3, 5.

  4. 4

    Download

    The rotated PDF downloads when the job finishes.

Tips and best practices

  • If a page was already rotated, the new angle is added to that rotation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate a single page?

Yes. Turn off All pages and select that page.

Does this flatten the PDF?

No. It changes the page rotation flag and keeps the existing text and images.

Is the file uploaded?

No. The rotation is applied in your browser.
