About the Image Compressor

The Image Compressor reduces the file size of JPEG, PNG and WebP images directly in your browser using the Canvas API. Nothing is uploaded, so you can compress a photo of an ID document without it ever leaving your device. You choose the quality level and see the exact before-and-after size, which makes it easy to hit a specific limit such as a 2 MB email attachment cap or a 1 MB upload requirement on a job application portal.

Where people use the Image Compressor

  • Getting a photo under an upload limit on a form or job portal.
  • Reducing the size of images before emailing them to a client.
  • Speeding up a web page by shrinking its hero images.
  • Preparing images for upload to a marketplace that rejects large files.

How to use the Image Compressor

  1. 1

    Select an image

    Drop a file onto the tool or click to browse. JPG, PNG and WebP are supported.

  2. 2

    Set the quality

    Around 80 is visually lossless for most photos. Drop to 60 or below when you need a much smaller file.

  3. 3

    Compare and download

    Review the size difference, then download the compressed image.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Compression runs locally via the browser Canvas API. This is why there is no file size cap and why it works offline.

How much smaller will the file get?

A typical photo shrinks by 60 to 80 percent at quality 80. Screenshots with large flat colour areas can compress even further.

Does compressing a photo reduce its resolution?

No. The dimensions stay exactly the same; only the encoding quality changes. Use the Image Resizer when you need to change dimensions.

Why is my PNG bigger after compression?

PNG is already lossless. Re-encoding it rarely helps, so convert it to JPEG or WebP instead, both of which the tool supports.
