About the Rotate Image

Rotate Image is for a picture that landed on its side, upside down, or mirrored. Each click turns it left or right by 90 degrees, or flips it horizontally or vertically, and you can combine a turn and a flip. The preview updates as you click, and that preview is the file you download. It does not offer 15 degrees or any other free angle, because a partial turn would add empty corners.

JPEG, PNG, WebP, and BMP keep their format when the browser can write it. GIF becomes a still PNG of the first frame. One picture at a time. Nothing is uploaded, and no account is required. A 90 degree turn swaps width and height. Crop after you rotate if the subject is sideways and you also need a region.

Where people use the Rotate Image

  • Turning a phone photo that landed on its side.
  • Mirroring a screenshot.
  • Flipping a scan that came in upside down.

How to use the Rotate Image

  1. 1

    Choose an image

    The original appears in the preview.

  2. 2

    Rotate or flip

    Rotate left, rotate right, flip horizontal, or flip vertical. You can combine a turn and a flip.

  3. 3

    Check the output size

    A 90° turn swaps width and height.

  4. 4

    Download

    The file matches the preview.

Tips and best practices

  • Rotate before you crop if the subject is sideways.
  • Each download is a new encode. Keep the original if you might rotate again.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate by 15 degrees?

No. This page only turns in 90° steps, plus flips. A free angle would add empty corners and is not offered.

Is the picture uploaded?

No.

Does GIF animation survive?

No. The download is a still PNG of the first frame, and the page says so.
