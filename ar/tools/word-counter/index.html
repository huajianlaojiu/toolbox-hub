About the

The Word Counter gives you a live count of words, characters, sentences and paragraphs as you type, along with reading time and speaking time estimates. It also surfaces the average word length and the count of unique words, which is what you need when a client specifies a limit and you want to know how far you are from it. Counting happens on every keystroke locally, so a 50,000-word document stays responsive.

Where people use the

  • Checking whether an article meets a client word limit.
  • Estimating how long a speech or voice-over will take to read.
  • Counting characters for a form field with a hard limit.
  • Measuring the length of a meta description before publishing.

How to use the

  1. 1

    Type or paste your text

    The counts update as you type, with no button to press.

  2. 2

    Read the statistics

    Check words, characters, sentences, paragraphs and the reading and speaking time estimates.

  3. 3

    Use the reading time estimate

    Speaking time is calculated at roughly 130 words per minute, which is close to a normal presentation pace.

Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

It assumes about 225 words per minute for silent reading and about 130 words per minute for speaking aloud.

Does it count spaces in the character count?

Yes. The character count includes spaces, and a separate count without spaces is shown alongside it.

How are sentences counted?

A sentence ends at a period, question mark or exclamation mark. Abbreviations such as e.g. or Dr. are counted as separate sentences.
