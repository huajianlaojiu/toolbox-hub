About the Resize Video

Resize Video changes a clip's resolution in your browser and downloads an MP4. Presets cover 1080p, 720p, 480p, and 360p, and you can type a custom width and height. Keep aspect ratio is on by default, so the picture is scaled to fit that frame instead of being stretched. A portrait clip uses a portrait frame for those presets.

A larger frame does not add detail. When the output is bigger than the original, the page says the upscale will not add real detail. Encoding uses ffmpeg in this tab. Nothing is uploaded. A long video, or a phone with little free memory, can be slow or fail. The first run downloads the ffmpeg engine, about 25 MB, and later runs reuse it.

Where people use the Resize Video

  • Making a 1080p clip into 720p before sending it.
  • Fitting a landscape video into a smaller 16:9 frame.
  • Checking a custom width and height without installing an editor.

How to use the Resize Video

  1. 1

    Add a video

    Choose a file. It stays on this device. The page reads the source width and height from the preview.

  2. 2

    Pick a size

    Use 1080p, 720p, 480p, or 360p, or type a width and height. Keep aspect ratio locks the other side to the original shape.

  3. 3

    Check the output line

    The output line is the even size that will be encoded. If it is larger than the source, the note says upscaling will not add real detail.

  4. 4

    Resize and download

    The MP4 is encoded locally. Keep the tab open. Download it when the preview of the result appears.

Dicas e práticas recomendadas

  • Leave keep aspect ratio on unless you mean to stretch the picture.
  • If you need a smaller file as well as a smaller frame, compress the result afterwards.

Perguntas frequentes

Will a bigger preset make a soft video look sharp?

No. Upscaling only stretches the frames you already have. It does not recover detail. The page warns you when the output is larger than the source.

Is the file uploaded?

No. ffmpeg runs in your browser. The only download is the ffmpeg engine itself, the first time you use a video tool in this browser.

Why is the size slightly different from what I typed?

The H.264 encoder needs even width and height. The output line shows the size that will actually be written.

Can a phone resize a long video?

Sometimes. Large or long videos can be slow or run out of memory, especially on a phone. A short clip on a desktop is the comfortable case.
