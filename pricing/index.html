Toolbox Hub Pro

Keep using every tool for a typical single job. Pro is there when you want a larger batch, a file of the job list this browser already keeps, and no ads. Prices are in US dollars.

Free

$0

  • Every current tool for one file, one conversion, or one calculation.
  • Up to 3 files when merging PDFs, combining images into a PDF, packing a ZIP or TAR, or merging videos.
  • One video frame, or a batch of 8.
  • The latest 20 job summaries, readable on this page.
  • Ads can show once an ad slot is configured.

Pro

$6 / month

or $48/year, which is $4/month

  • Up to 50 files for PDF merge, images to PDF, and archives.
  • Video merge up to 10 files, the limit this tool already uses.
  • Frame batches up to 30.
  • Download the job list as JSON.
  • No ads on this browser while Pro is active.
  • Priority email support — coming next. There is no staffed queue yet.

Monthly checkout is not connected. Set NEXT_PUBLIC_PRO_CHECKOUT_MONTHLY_URL and rebuild. This page cannot charge a card.

Yearly checkout is not connected. Set NEXT_PUBLIC_PRO_CHECKOUT_YEARLY_URL and rebuild.

Not included, on either plan: cloud storage, an account, server-side processing, an API, or team seats. Files keep being processed in your browser.

Checkout, once connected, is a Lemon Squeezy hosted page. They are the merchant of record and add any applicable tax. Cancel or update the card from the receipt they email. Toolbox Hub never sees the card number.

The steps to connect checkout, the verify endpoint, and the success URL are in the repository file docs/pro-billing.md.

Already subscribed?

Paste the license key from the receipt email. The key is checked by the verify endpoint, then a Pro flag is saved in this browser only. Tool inputs are not part of that request.

Activation is waiting on NEXT_PUBLIC_LICENSE_VERIFY_URL. Until that HTTPS endpoint is set at build time, this form cannot turn Pro on.

Questions

Do I have to pay to use a tool?

No. A typical single job stays free: one file, one conversion, or one calculation, with no account. Pro is optional and does not sit in front of that path.

What is capped on the free plan?

PDF merge, images combined into a PDF, ZIP or TAR packing, and video merge include 3 files at a time. Frame capture includes one frame or a batch of 8. You can read the job list on this browser. The button on the tool runs the free portion instead of stopping the tool.

What does Pro add?

Pro raises those batches to 50 files for PDFs, images, and archives, and to 10 videos. Frame batches go up to 30. You can download the job list as JSON, and ads stay off on this browser. Priority email support is marked as coming next and is not a staffed queue yet.

Where do my files go?

They stay in this browser, the same as before. Checkout happens on the merchant's hosted page. The license check sends only the key you paste. The job list stores a one-line summary, not the file.

Why is checkout not taking a card here?

This site is a static export. Card numbers are never entered on Toolbox Hub. When the Lemon Squeezy checkout links are set at build time, the buttons go to that hosted page. Until then there is nothing to pay and nothing to cancel.
