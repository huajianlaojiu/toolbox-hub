Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what data Toolbox Hub ("the Site", "we") handles, why we handle it, and what you can do about it. We have kept it in plain language on purpose.

1. The short version

Most tools process your input inside your browser and do not upload it to a server. The Site itself does, however, load third-party resources: advertising and audience measurement (only after you consent), JavaScript engines and language models from a CDN for some tools, and a small number of embedded third-party applications that make their own network requests. These are described in sections 4 and 5 below.

2. Information you provide

The tools on this Site do not require an account and we do not ask you to submit personal information to use them. If you email us through the contact page, we will keep your message and your email address so that we can reply and keep a record of the issue. We do not add correspondents to a marketing list.

3. Files you select

When you use a tool such as the image compressor, the image resizer or the watermark remover, the file is read by JavaScript running in your browser. For these tools the file is not transmitted to a server we operate and is discarded when you close or refresh the page. Some tools need to download a processing engine or language model from a third-party CDN before they can work, so they require an internet connection on first use. A few embedded third-party applications handle data inside an iframe under their own policies.

4. Advertising and cookies

This Site displays advertising provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads and to measure their performance.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • We show our own consent banner before any advertising or analytics script is loaded. Advertising and analytics cookies are only used if you choose to accept them, and you can review or withdraw your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer. This applies to all visitors, including those in Europe, the UK and Switzerland.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and other sites. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page, and you can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services in the Google partner sites privacy page.

Third-party services and external requests

To be precise about what this Site loads: advertising is served by Google AdSense and its script is only requested after you accept advertising cookies; some tools download processing engines and language models (FFmpeg WebAssembly, Tesseract.js, JSZip, lamejs) from the unpkg CDN on first use; and a small number of embedded third-party applications run in an iframe and may contact their own APIs and services. Those third parties have their own privacy policies. Fonts are self-hosted from this domain.

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Standard access logs (IP address, browser type, referring page and request time) are collected by the hosting provider under its own policy; we do not have access to a user-level analytics dataset from those logs. The only audience measurement we receive is the consent-based Google Analytics 4 data described in section 5.

5. Analytics and server logs

Like almost every website, the hosting provider records standard access logs such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and the time of the request. These logs are used to keep the Site available, to diagnose errors, and to detect abuse. They are not used to build a profile of you.

If you allow analytics cookies, the Site also uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure which pages and tools are visited. GA4 receives the page URL, browser and device information, a cookie or device identifier, and an approximate location derived from your IP address; it does not receive the text or files you enter into a tool, and raw IP addresses are not made available to us. If you do not allow analytics cookies, the GA4 script is never loaded. You can withdraw consent at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer.

6. Local storage and cookies

The Site uses browser local storage to remember your selected theme, your language preference, the full text you type into the teleprompter tool, and your cookie consent choice. This data stays in your browser and is not sent to us; you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

7. Legal basis

Where the GDPR or comparable legislation applies, we rely on legitimate interest for basic operation and security of the Site, and on consent for advertising and any non-essential cookies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Because we do not maintain user accounts and do not collect data from the tools themselves, most requests are handled simply by contacting us. For advertising data held by Google, you can manage your preferences through the Google Ads Settings page linked above.

9. International transfers

Advertising providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by the provider's own safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Children

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page always reflects the current version, and material changes will be summarised here.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to exercise your rights, can be sent to hello@toolbox-hub.io. See also our contact page.
